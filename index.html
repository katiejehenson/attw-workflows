How Your Workflows Work Together

A plain-English guide to the two-layer workflow system, what each workflow does, when to apply it, and what happens automatically vs. what you do manually.

The Big Picture
Two Layers, Working Together
Your entire workflow system is built in two layers. Layer 1 — All Trips workflows fire for every single client, every time, no matter the destination. Layer 2 — Trip Type workflows layer on top for cruises, Disney, FIT trips, and more. You never repeat universal steps inside a trip-specific workflow — Layer 1 already handles them.
Layer 1 — Every Trip
  • 01  All Trips: Inbound Leads
  • 02  All Trips: Planning
  • 03  All Trips: Payments
  • 04  All Trips: Booked (Pre + Post)
Layer 2 — Trip Type (runs alongside)
  • 🚢  Mass Market Cruise
  • 🏰  Walt Disney World
  • 🚢  Disney Cruise Line
  • 🗺  FIT / Custom Itinerary
  • + any future trip types
Right now, most workflows are applied manually. The goal is auto-apply within the next few weeks. In the meantime: when a trip moves to Planning, manually apply the relevant Layer 2 workflow. When a trip is marked Booked, All Trips: Payments and All Trips: Booked will apply automatically.

All Trips Workflows — One by One
Four workflows that run for every client. Each covers a distinct stage of the trip. Here's exactly what's inside each one.

01
All Trips: Inbound Leads
Trigger: Trip status → New Lead
Apply Manually
Walks you through the first contact with a new client — from scheduling a discovery call through sending the call recap and getting the agency agreement signed. Email notifications for this workflow are turned off so they don't fire unexpectedly.
Tasks (you do these)
  • Schedule discovery call
  • Send call recap email (manual)
  • Send agency client agreement + planning fee
Automations
  • Email notifications off for this workflow — you control all sends
When does this fire? The trigger is trip status = New Lead — not trip creation. This means it only fires when you intentionally move a trip to New Lead, giving you full control over timing.
02
All Trips: Planning
Trigger: Trip status → Planning
Auto-Applies ✓
Fires as soon as the trip status changes to Planning — whether you drag the trip over in the pipeline view or change it from the dropdown. This is the workflow that kicks off active planning. The assumption here is that a trip moves to Planning because the client has signed the agreement and paid the planning fee.
Tasks (you do these)
  • Contact the DMC / preferred supplier
  • Create and send the proposal
  • Proposal follow-up if no response
  • Proposal updates as needed
Automations (fire on their own)
  • Immediately: Tern account setup email sent to client
  • 5 days later: Travel insurance introductory email
  • When client clicks "Approve Itinerary": Proposal approved email fires (only if they click it — not if you approve on their behalf)
Travel insurance email: This is a tap on the shoulder, not a hard sell. It includes a line in bold that says a preliminary quote will be included with the proposal. It is not delivering the quote itself — just setting the expectation that insurance will be discussed.

It can be manually attached to the proposal email or sent separately. Let us know your preference so we can add it to the right touchpoint.
03
All Trips: Payments
Trigger: Trip status → Booked
Auto-Applies ✓
Fires as soon as a trip is marked Booked. Handles the entire payment lifecycle — from deposit through final payment. Because this is centralized here, you do not need payment reminder emails inside any trip-specific workflow (Disney, cruise, FIT). It's already covered.
Tasks (you do these)
  • Enter commission info with host agency
  • Mark trip as booked in Tern
  • Post-trip: verify commission has been received
Automations (fire on their own)
  • 10 days before payment due: Payment reminder email to client
  • 1 day before payment due: Final reminder email
  • When client authorizes payment: Acknowledgment email — "I'll go ahead and pay this for you"
  • Supplier payment task fires alongside the 10-day reminder
How Tern knows the payment dates: Payment due dates live inside the booking or package record in Tern. This workflow watches those dates and fires the reminder emails automatically. Make sure dates are entered in Tern when you set up the booking.
04
All Trips: Booked (Pre + Post Travel)
Trigger: Trip status → Booked · Runs alongside all trip-type workflows
Auto-Applies ✓
The universal pre- and post-travel workflow. Trip-type agnostic — works the same whether the client is on a cruise, a Disney trip, or a custom FIT. When you apply a Disney Cruise, WDW, or FIT workflow, this one is already running alongside it. You never need to repeat these steps inside a trip-specific workflow.
Tasks (you do these)
  • 7 days after task creation: Add trip-specific tasks as needed — tickets, car rental, anything outside the standard list
  • 10 days after trip ends: Review trip feedback
  • 181 days after trip ends: Archive the trip
Automations — all Active ✓
  • 48-Hour Bon Voyage — 2 days before trip starts. Tern app link, itinerary link, online check-in reminder. Active
  • Welcome Home Survey — 2 days after trip ends. Feedback survey + photo request. Active
  • 6-Month Post-Travel Check-In — 180 days after trip ends. Re-engagement email, plants the seed for the next trip. Active
Arrival Day Check-In has moved. It now lives in the FIT Booked workflow (Active) rather than here, since it's most relevant for land-based independent travel. Cruise clients get the Bon Voyage and Welcome Home emails from this workflow.
❓ Question for Christine: Do you regularly schedule pre-travel calls with clients? If so, we can add an automated email 30 days out asking them to book time with you for a trip review call. Let us know and we'll build it.
The Golden Rule — What You Never Need to Repeat
  • 1
    Commission tracking is in All Trips: Payments. Don't add a GRASP task to your Disney or FIT workflow.
  • 2
    Payment reminders (10-day, 1-day, authorization) are in All Trips: Payments. Trip-specific workflows don't need their own.
  • 3
    Bon Voyage, welcome home survey, and 6-month follow-up are in All Trips: Booked. The Arrival Day Check-In lives in FIT Booked only. Never duplicate any of these in other trip-type workflows.
  • 4
    Pre-travel document wrap-up (22 days out) is in All Trips: Booked. Your Disney and cruise workflows don't need their own version.

06. Mass Market: Cruise Booked (TBB)
For: Royal Caribbean, Norwegian, Holland America, Princess, Celebrity, etc. · Auto-apply tag: Mass Market Cruise
Tag-Based Auto-Apply
Based on the Travel Biz Boss workflow, modified to match your writing style and trimmed of tasks that don't apply to your process. Auto-applies when the Mass Market Cruise trip tag is added. Payment reminders and post-travel follow-up are handled by All Trips Payments and Booked — no duplication needed here.

  • 0 days: Double-check automations — inactive/active and due dates
  • 0 days: Edit & ACTIVATE Online Check-In Reminder
  • 0 days: Move trip to Booked status if not already done
  • 0 days: Edit & ACTIVATE Initial Insurance Quote Automation (remove if N/A)
  • 0 days: Are there any special concerns / celebrations?
  • 0 days: Review / Update Bon Voyage automation with destination-specific email
  • 1 day after creation: Edit & ACTIVATE Insurance before Final Payment Automation
  • 135 days before trip: If you need to confirm transfers, ACTIVATE Flight Request Automation
  • 72 days before trip: If VISA/ESTA required, edit & ACTIVATE VISA Automation (remove — updated with agent below)
  • 35 days before trip: Double-check all amenities are showing on the reservation
  • 35 days before trip: Edit & ACTIVATE Final Documents Automation
  • 14 days before trip: Send Gift (do you send gifts?)
  • 6 days after trip ends: Send Final Payment email (when paid in full)
Automations
  • Initial Insurance Quote — 2 days after automation added (Inactive — activate per task)
  • 160-Day Pre-Cruise — 160 days before trip Active
  • Insurance Prompt before Final — 120 days before trip Active
  • Communication before Your Trip — 80 days before trip Active
  • Flight Request Required? — 70 days before trip Active
  • Are any VISAs or ETAs required? — 65 days before trip Active
  • Visa + Health Email Draft — 65 days before trip Active
  • 80-Day Pre-Cruise (includes online check-in reminder) — 60 days before trip Active
  • Money Matters: Cruise Edition — 45 days before trip Active
  • Final Documents & Itinerary Link — 33 days before trip Active
  • 30-Day Pre-Cruise — 30 days before trip Active
  • What NOT to Bring on a Cruise — 25 days before trip Active

  • Bon Voyage: Cruise — 5 days before trip Active

Several tasks require your attention at booking time — particularly activating the insurance, VISA, and flight request automations based on each client's situation. The task list walks you through exactly which ones to review and activate. The gift task (14 days before) should be removed if you don't send physical gifts.

FIT Booked
For: Independent travel, escorted tours, DMC-based itineraries · Trip tag: FIT

Covers everything unique to fully independent travel — final paperwork from suppliers, hotel confirmations, and a full pre-travel check-in sequence. This workflow is also where the Arrival Day Check-In now lives, as it's most relevant for land-based independent trips where local partners are on the ground.
Tasks (you do these)
  • 35 days before trip: FIT | Request Final Paperwork from Suppliers Email notification: On

  • 7 days before trip: FIT | Send Hotel Booking Confirmations to Property (if necessary) Notification: Off
Automations — all Active ✓
  • 90-Day Check-In — 90 days before trip starts. 1 email. Dining, spa, special experiences. Active

Arrival Day Check-In was moved here from All Trips: Booked because the language about "local partners" is specific to FIT trips. Cruise clients don't need this message — they're already on the ship.
